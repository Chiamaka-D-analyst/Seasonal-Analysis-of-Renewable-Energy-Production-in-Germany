# Seasonal-Analysis-of-Renewable-Energy-Production-in-Germany
Seasonal Analysis of Renewable Energy Production in Germany

# Objective:
To analyze monthly energy production trends and seasonal patterns of renewable energy sources in Germany to support energy planning and system reliability.


# Key Insights:
The time-series analysis indicates that solar energy exhibits the strongest seasonality, with peak production occurring during mid-year months (June and July) and significantly lower output during winter months (January–February and November–December). Wind energy also shows seasonal variation but consistently generates higher energy output than solar across most months. Biogas remains the most stable energy source throughout the year, showing minimal seasonal fluctuation. This stability makes biogas well-suited as a dispatchable backup energy source to complement intermittent renewable sources. A well-balanced energy mix integrating variable renewables with stable generation technologies is essential for maintaining a reliable and sustainable energy supply in Germany.
